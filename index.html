LSTM/GRU Stock Price Prediction

honest negative result github.com/eniscan17/lstm-stock-prediction

A from-scratch PyTorch time-series pipeline predicting next-day stock returns — then, instead of stopping at "the model beats a baseline," I tested whether it would actually make money as a real trading strategy. It doesn't, and this section shows that on purpose: catching an overclaim before it ships is the point.

total return, test period — strategy vs. buy & hold
The result that matters most: a small, statistically real RMSE edge over the naive baseline did not survive being turned into an actual trading strategy. After transaction costs, both models lost to simply buying and holding — verified across 5 random seeds, not one lucky run.
